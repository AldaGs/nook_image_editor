Nook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Nook (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a design editor for boards and social posts. This policy explains what data the app handles and what it does not do. Nook is local-first: your projects are created and stored on your own device, and we do not operate a server that collects or stores them.

1. Data you create

Your projects (documents, boards, layers and text), project thumbnails, the pictures you add, saved stickers and app settings are stored on your device in your browser’s storage (the Origin Private File System and local storage). Pictures are re-encoded on import, which strips photo metadata such as GPS location. This data never leaves your device unless you turn on Google Drive sync (see below). We do not have access to it.

2. On-device AI

AI features are off by default. When you turn them on, model files are downloaded to your device from the app’s own server or, for the larger BiRefNet models, from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), and the models run on your device. Your pictures are not uploaded anywhere for AI processing.

3. Optional Google Drive sync

Google Drive sync is an opt-in feature you turn on from the projects screen or Settings.

3.1 Google user data the app accesses

Google API used: Google Drive API v3.
OAuth scope requested: exactly one — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This scope grants access only to a hidden, application-specific folder (appDataFolder) inside your Google Drive. The folder is invisible in the regular Drive interface and inaccessible to any other application.

Within that folder, the app accesses only files it created itself:

  • projects.json — your project list (project ids, names and last-edited times).
  • docs/<id>/doc.json and docs/<id>/thumb.png — each project’s document and thumbnail.
  • assets/<hash> — the pictures used in your projects, named by a hash of their contents.
  • deleted.json — ids of projects you removed from Drive, so your other devices remove them too.

For these files the app reads only the Drive file ID, the file name and the file contents. It does not access other metadata (no owners, permissions, sharing, revisions or comments). Operations used: files.list (restricted to appDataFolder), files.create, files.update, files.get?alt=media and files.delete, all on the app’s own files.

Google user data the app does NOT access: any other file in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope makes this technically impossible), your Google profile, name, email address or photo, or any other Google service. The app requests no sensitive or restricted scopes.

3.2 How the app uses this data

  • Purpose: solely to keep your Nook projects in sync between your own devices and to let you recover them.
  • No server-side processing: all reads and writes go directly between your device and Google’s servers. We operate no backend that receives or proxies your Google user data.
  • No secondary use: we do not use Google user data to train AI/ML models, for advertising, analytics, profiling, or anything other than sync.

3.3 Storage and sharing

  • Storage: synced files live only in your Google Drive’s hidden app folder. We keep no copy.
  • Tokens: sign-in uses Google Identity Services. The short-lived access token (valid for about one hour) is kept only in your browser’s local storage on your device so the app stays signed in across reloads; it is never sent to a server we control, expires on its own, and is deleted when you turn sync off. We do not request or receive a refresh token.
  • Sharing: we do not transfer, sell, share or disclose Google user data to any third party.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Nook’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not run analytics or tracking on your projects.
  • We do not store your data on servers we operate.

6. Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Turn sync off on a device in Settings → Google Drive sync → “Turn off”.
  • Delete a single project from Drive and all your devices: delete it in the app and choose to remove it from Google Drive too.
  • Delete everything Nook keeps in your Drive with Settings → “Clear Nook data from Google Drive”, or in Google Drive’s settings under “Manage apps”.
  • Revoke the app’s access entirely at your Google Account permissions page.
  • Clearing the app’s site data in your browser removes everything stored on that device.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes are reflected by the “Last updated” date above.
