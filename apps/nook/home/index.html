What Nook does

  • Create boards and social posts with shapes, text, pictures and stickers, including Instagram-size boards with safe-zone guides.
  • Organise work into projects, each with its own boards and layers.
  • Export finished boards as images, or save whole projects as .nook files.
  • Optional on-device AI tools, such as background removal, that run entirely on your device.
  • Works offline and can be installed as an app on phones, tablets and computers.

Your data stays yours

Nook is local-first: your projects are stored on your own device, and we run no server that collects them. There are no ads and no tracking.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose to turn on sync, Nook keeps a copy of your projects in a private, hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so the same projects show up on all your devices. Nook asks for a single permission, drive.appdata, which only lets it see the files it creates in that folder. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive, and it does not access your name, email or any other Google data. You can turn sync off, delete Nook’s Drive data from Settings, or revoke access from your Google Account at any time.
